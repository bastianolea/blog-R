[Este tutorial](https://bastianolea.rbind.io/blog/quarto_blog/) se escribió con apoyo del asistente de IA [Posit Assistant](https://bastianolea.rbind.io/blog/posit_assistant/) en modo planificación bajo el modelo Claude Sonnet 5, por medio de un [prompt extenso](https://github.com/bastianolea/blog-R/blob/master/content/blog/quarto_blog/prompt.md) que instruyó al LLM a usar mi _skill_ de [estilo personal de escritura](https://github.com/bastianolea/blog-R/blob/master/.posit/assistant/skills/estilo-escritura/SKILL.md), mi _skill_ de comprensión del [funcionamiento de mi blog Hugo](https://github.com/bastianolea/blog-R/blob/master/.posit/assistant/skills/hugo-blog/SKILL.md), la [transcripción](https://github.com/bastianolea/blog-R/blob/master/content/blog/quarto_blog/transcripcion_taller.vtt) del taller en vivo generada por Zoom (que derivó en un [resumen de la transcripción](https://github.com/bastianolea/blog-R/blob/master/content/blog/quarto_blog/resumen_transcripcion_taller.md)), las [diapositivas Quarto RevealJS](https://github.com/SantiagoRusers/taller_quarto_blog) del taller, y el código de un [tutorial anterior](https://github.com/bastianolea/blog-R/blob/master/content/blog/tutorial_quarto_github_pages/index.md) sobre Quarto (pero de una temática más amplia). De todo esto resultó una [planificación](https://github.com/bastianolea/blog-R/blob/master/.posit/assistant/plans/2026-08-06-1716-plan.md) de redacción, la cual modifiqué manualmente para que Sonnet 5 la implementara con el modo `/yolo` de Posit Assistant, con una instrucción para que dejara comentarios en partes donde falten contenidos o existan dudas (para que yo las resolviera). Finalmente, revisé el texto resultante completo, y modifiqué todas las secciones agregando comentarios, mejorando la escritura, y agregando ejemplos. Básicamente era un caso de uso perfecto de IA porque tenía un marco muy específico de documentos, instrucciones y contexto para obtener el resultado deseado.

So I wanted to share my experience on jumping to the dark side of content generation. I recently [did a workshop on creating a Quarto blog](https://santiagorusers.github.io/posts/taller_julio_2026/), and I wanted to write a detailed tutorial afterwards. I lacked the time and energy to do it properly, but on the contrary, I had plenty of material for the tutorial itself: a previous, broader tutorial on a similar topic, the workshop's voice transcript, and the Quarto slides I used during the workshop. I also happened to have a personal writing style AI skill, and a Hugo blog skill too. 

I happen to [run a blog](https://bastianolea.rbind.io/blog/) for R teaching and tips, and I enjoy writing for it, devoting time to create content for others to learn. But this was basically the perfect scenario for AI-assisted writing, as I had a very specific framework of documents, instructions, and context to obtain the desired result.

So, [this Quarto blog tutorial](https://bastianolea.rbind.io/blog/quarto_blog/) was written with the support of [Posit Assistant](https://bastianolea.rbind.io/blog/posit_assistant/) in planning mode under the Claude Sonnet 5 model, through an [extensive prompt](https://github.com/bastianolea/blog-R/blob/master/content/blog/quarto_blog/prompt.md) that instructed the LLM to use my _skill_ for [personal writing style](https://github.com/bastianolea/blog-R/blob/master/.posit/assistant/skills/estilo-escritura/SKILL.md), my _skill_ for understanding [how my Hugo blog works](https://github.com/bastianolea/blog-R/blob/master/.posit/assistant/skills/hugo-blog/SKILL.md), the [transcript](https://github.com/bastianolea/blog-R/blob/master/content/blog/quarto_blog/transcripcion_taller.vtt) of the live workshop generated by Zoom (which led to a [summary of the transcript](https://github.com/bastianolea/blog-R/blob/master/content/blog/quarto_blog/resumen_transcripcion_taller.md)), the [Quarto RevealJS slides](https://github.com/SantiagoRusers/taller_quarto_blog) from the workshop, and the code from a [previous tutorial](https://github.com/bastianolea/blog-R/blob/master/content/blog/tutorial_quarto_github_pages/index.md) about Quarto (but on a broader topic). All of this resulted in a writing [plan](https://github.com/bastianolea/blog-R/blob/master/.posit/assistant/plans/2026-08-06-1716-plan.md), which I manually modified further, so that Sonnet 5 could implement it later using Posit Assistant's `/yolo` mode. 

This was all in preparation for the actual writing, as context is everything for these kind of tasks. For the actual writing, I gave the LLM a [second prompt](https://github.com/bastianolea/blog-R/blob/master/content/blog/quarto_blog/prompt.md) with an explicit instruction to leave HTML-style comments for me in sections of the post where content was missing, when more stuff could be clarified, or if any doubts/questions remained (both from the workshop assistants and from the LLM), with the objective of resolving these issues myself later. 

Finally, I reviewed the full resulting text, and edited all sections by adding comments, improving the overall writing, and adding more examples. The LLM-generated text was very spot-on on my expectations, and it helped me a lot to get the work to an almost-done state, based on the content I already had. It was also great that all content was text bases (slides, tutorials, transcripts), so all went smoothly. In my opinion it was a balanced AI writing experience, and it kinda changed my perspective on AI assist for text generation. Is solidified to me that the more context you give (as in, very long and detailed prompts, a lot of documents for review, even links to further knowledge), the better results you get, and in my case, the results were great!
